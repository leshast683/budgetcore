About Budgetly

A focused project built to make personal finance simple.

Budgetly is a personal finance tracker designed to give users a clear and honest view of their money. The goal is straightforward — help people log their expenses, stay within their budget, and understand where their money goes through simple summaries and visual charts.

This project is built around the core idea that managing your finances should not require complicated tools or financial expertise. Everything in Budgetly is focused on being practical, easy to use, and genuinely useful in day-to-day life.
