Frequently asked questions

How do I reset my password?

On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password?" and enter your email. You'll receive a link to set a new password. If you signed up with Apple or Google, use that same button to sign in instead — there's no separate BudgetCore password to reset.

How do I delete my account and data?

Open the app, go to Profile, and look for the delete account option under Account Settings. This permanently removes your profile, transactions, goals, investments, and net worth data. This can't be undone.

Is my financial data safe?

Yes. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and only accessible through your own authenticated account — no one else can see it, and we never sell it. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do live investment prices work?

When you add a stock or crypto symbol to a holding, BudgetCore looks up its current market price automatically whenever you open the Investments tab. Holdings without a symbol keep whatever price you entered manually.

Why didn't I get a push notification?

Make sure notifications are allowed for BudgetCore in your device's Settings app under Notifications. You can also check that you granted permission when first asked inside the app.

I found a bug or have a feature request — what do I do?

Email us at budgetcore20@gmail.com with as much detail as you can — what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. Screenshots help a lot.

Can I use BudgetCore on both the website and the iOS app?

Yes — sign in with the same account on budgetcore.net and the iOS app, and your data stays in sync across both.
