Privacy Policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

BudgetCore ("we", "our", "us") provides a personal finance tracker available at budgetcore.net and as an iOS app. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and name, used to create and secure your account.
  • Financial data you enter: transactions, budgets, savings and investment goals, and related amounts and categories you add to track your finances.
  • Profile photos: an optional photo for your profile or a savings/investment goal, taken with your camera or chosen from your photo library.
  • Authentication data: if you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name and email address from that provider to create your account.

How we use your information

We use your information solely to operate BudgetCore: to display your budgets and transactions, calculate spending trends and goal progress, generate AI-powered budgeting insights, and keep your account secure. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

AI-generated insights

To provide spending predictions and budgeting recommendations, summarized transaction and budget data may be sent to a third-party AI provider for processing. This data is used only to generate your insights and is not used to train AI models.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored with Supabase, our database and authentication provider, using industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest. Only you can access your financial data through your authenticated account.

Data sharing

We do not share your personal or financial information with third parties, except with service providers (such as our database host and AI provider) strictly to operate the app, or if required by law.

Your choices

  • You can edit or delete your transactions, goals, and profile information at any time from within the app.
  • You can delete your account and all associated data from the Profile screen.
  • Camera and photo library access are only requested when you choose to add a photo, and only used for that purpose.

Children's privacy

BudgetCore is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Contact us at budgetcore20@gmail.com.
